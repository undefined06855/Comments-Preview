# Comments Preview
by [undefined0](user:13351341)

---

Shows you the top comments of a level when searching for levels, similar to the Level Thumbnails mod.

The top five comments will scroll through next to the level name, showing the commentor's icon and the amount of likes
the comment got next to their comment.

Thank you to [dankmeme01](https://github.com/dankmeme01/) for providing a server to host the mod on!
